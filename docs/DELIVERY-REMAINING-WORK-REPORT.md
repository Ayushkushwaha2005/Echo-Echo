## 2. Verified working (with evidence)

Partner registration and approval; assignment with first-accept-wins; both handover codes end to end; customer tracking with the privacy rules; GPS reporting rules and consent; stale-position display; campus geofence for destinations; customer and admin maps; partner earnings from the ledger; incident and deduction workflow; audit logging. Evidence: the server tests named above ran today and passed; the browser journeys named above passed in CI on `main` with emulated phones.

## 3. Partially working

Offer decline (absent), background location (web limits), spoofing (bounds only), settlements (manual rail only, provider rail untested), failed deliveries (staff-resolved only), notifications (in-app only), mobile (emulated only), café hours (Chai Garam's all-day override from the 11 Nov test is still live in production).

## 4. Missing functionality

1. Route calculation and ETA (deliberately deferred).
2. A partner-side "cannot deliver" action with a reason, re-offer to another partner, and a customer-facing notice while an order is stuck.
3. Push notifications (no provider), SMS (no provider): a partner only learns of an offer while the app is open and polling.
4. Movement-plausibility checks between consecutive fixes (speed, teleport) and any device integrity signal.
5. Native background location (not possible in a web app; a packaged app would be needed).
6. Partner-facing earnings statement export and payout self-service through a provider (the only rail is a manual transfer recorded by an administrator).

## 5. Failed tests

None in the suites that ran today. See the run record in `docs/PLATFORM-AUDIT-CROSSCHECK-2026-11.md` §15.

## 6. Security and privacy gaps

- A plausible fabricated position within the bounds (near the café, recent, precise) is accepted; the partner's own position is not checked against the campus boundary. Risk: a partner can appear to be delivering while elsewhere. Mitigation today: handover codes, not positions, decide the money.
- The café sees the partner's first name only; the customer sees first name, photo and rating; no phone or surname crosses (tested). Unchanged.
- Position rows are deleted with the end of carrying and the consent row is closed; data export contains no position. Unchanged.
- The admin position-view audit is throttled to one row per 5 minutes per administrator and order; a sustained watch is therefore recorded as a series of rows, not every poll. Acceptable, documented here.
- No real-phone verification of the permission prompts, revocation mid-delivery, lock screen or airplane mode (the `DELIVERY-TRACKING.md` checklist is still entirely unchecked).

## 7. Controlled campus pilot readiness

**Ready for a controlled pilot with human fallbacks, not for unattended operation.** What makes it workable: the money path does not depend on GPS (codes do), every state change is audited, the customer is told honestly when no partner is available or the position is unknown, and cancellations before pickup refund automatically. What the pilot needs around it:

1. A person watching Campus Control's Live Deliveries and Support during service hours, because a stuck delivery is resolved only by staff and a partner is only told of an offer while the app is open.
2. Two real phones through the `DELIVERY-TRACKING.md` checklist before relying on tracking for anything a customer is promised.
3. Restore Chai Garam's hours and decide Frisco's status before the pilot's first day.
4. A written rule for "could not deliver" (who refunds, who re-offers) until the product has one.
5. Payout: the manual bank transfer rail, with the UTR recorded in Campus Control, is the only one; tell partners the payout day and keep it.
