# Café QR — readiness and verification procedure

Status (2026-10-22): **waiting for the owner's café QR image.** Nothing has been
invented or substituted: no QR has been generated for the café, and no URL has
been guessed.

## What the image must be checked for, in order

1. **Keep the original.** Save the file exactly as supplied under
   `brand/qr/supplied/` (never a screenshot of it, never re-encoded) and record
   its sha256. Every later step reads this file; nothing modifies it.
2. **Decode and classify it** (read-only):

   ```bash
   python -m pip install opencv-python-headless==4.10.0.84
   python tools/qr-verify.py brand/qr/supplied/<file> --expect https://www.echoecho.tech/web/
   ```

   The report gives the decoded text and one of:

   | kind | meaning | what happens |
   |---|---|---|
   | `site_url` | an https link | Its host must be `www.echoecho.tech` (or the apex). Exit 0 only if it matches `--expect` (or an allowed host) **and** the quiet zone is ≥ 4 modules. |
   | `upi_payment` | a static `upi://pay` QR (payee VPA and name shown) | **Not used for checkout.** ECHO ECHO orders are online-prepaid through Cashfree and confirmed server-to-server; a static UPI QR cannot be tied to an order, a webhook or a refund. It may only be displayed as the café's own counter QR, outside the app. |
   | `other` | plain text, `http://`, another scheme | Not usable as a menu/site QR. |

3. **Quiet zone.** ISO/IEC 18004 asks for 4 light modules on every side. The
   tool measures it from the image. If the supplied artwork crops it, do not
   crop or redraw the code: add white margin around the original artwork.
4. **Destination.** For a menu/site QR, open the decoded URL on a phone and
   confirm it reaches the café's menu over HTTPS with no redirect to another
   host. The URL in the report is the evidence, not what the poster says.
5. **Print test** (needs a person and the printed copy):
   - iPhone (Camera app) and Android (Google Lens or the camera) — both;
   - at counter distance (about 1 m) and at queue distance (about 2 m);
   - under the café's real lighting, and at a 30–45° angle;
   - record: phone model, distance, decoded URL, pass/fail, date.
6. **Record** the final destination, the sha256 of the original, the tool
   report and the print-test table in this file.

## Verified on the existing site QR (control sample)

`brand/qr/echoecho-web-qr.png` (generated by `tools/qr-make.py`):
decodes to `https://www.echoecho.tech/web/`, 33 modules, quiet zone 4 modules,
decodes at 600/300/150/100 px — `usable_as_menu_qr: true`. A copy with the
border cropped away is refused (quiet zone 1). A synthetic `upi://pay` QR is
classified `upi_payment` and refused as a menu QR.
